1. GIAO DIEN DAU TRANG VA THANH RELOAD ROLE

Phia tren cung cua ban dieu khien gom 2 thanh cong cu chinh:

Cac nut chuyen Tab vong thi:

  • [HE THONG]: Mo man hinh cai dat tong the, quan ly cau hoi va kiem tra ket noi.
  • [XUAT PHAT]: Mo ban dieu khien vong 1 (Khoi dong 60s).
  • [RA KHOI]: Mo ban dieu khien vong 2 (Tang toc, thang diem 40-30-20-10).
  • [VUOT SONG]: Mo ban dieu khien vong 3 (Hang ngang va Tu khoa, thang diem 50-10).
  • [VINH QUANG]: Mo ban dieu khien vong 4 (Ve dich, goi cau hoi va ngoi sao).
  • [CAU HOI PHU]: Mo giao dien cau hoi phu phan dinh thang thua.

Cac nut tren thanh RELOAD ROLE (Tai lai tu xa khong mat du lieu):

  • [TAT CA]: Gui lenh F5 tai lai toan bo tat ca man hinh dang ket noi.
  • [4 TS]: Gui lenh tai lai dong thoi ca 4 may thi sinh 1, 2, 3, 4.
  • [TS1], [TS2], [TS3], [TS4]: Tai lai rieng biet may cua tung thi sinh neu gap truc trac.
  • [MC]: Tai lai man hinh cua Nguoi dan chuong trinh.
  • [MAY CHIEU]: Tai lai man hinh may chieu san khau (Projector).
  • [GRAPHIC]: Tai lai man hinh do hoa livestream / OBS.
  • [TAI PDF] va [HUONG DAN]: Mo va tai tai lieu huong dan nay.

2. TAB 0: HE THONG - CHUAN BI TRUOC TRAN DAU

B1

Nhap danh sach thi sinh

Dien Ho ten, Truong/Lop vao 4 o: TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4 tai khung Thong Tin Thi Sinh.

-> Thao tac: Nhap chu truc tiep vao o, sau do bam nut [Luu du lieu].
B2

Nap file cau hoi Excel

Bam nut [Tai file mau Excel] ve may neu chua co mau. Dien day du de thi 4 vong. Bam nut [Chon tep], chon file .xlsx roi bam nut [Nap file cau hoi Excel].

-> Thao tac: Kiem tra thong bao thanh cong xuat hien tren man hinh.
B3

Ket noi may thi sinh

Huong dan 4 thi sinh quet ma QR tuong ung hoac truy cap link hien thi, nhap Room Code va PIN vi tri.

-> Thao tac: Quan sat den bao TS1, TS2, TS3, TS4 chuyen sang mau XANH LA CAY la ket noi tot.
B4

Bat dau vong thi

Khi MC doc loi mo dau vong thi nao, bam nut [Bat dau vong thi] tuong ung de tat ca cac man hinh chuyen canh dong bo.

3. VONG 1: XUAT PHAT (KHOI DONG 60 GIAY)

Moi thi sinh co 60 giay tra loi goi cau hoi ca nhan. Dung moi cau duoc +10 diem, sai khong bi tru diem.

B1

Chon thi sinh thi dau

Bam vao the thi sinh [Thi sinh 1] (hoac 2, 3, 4) o hang tren cung cua Tab Xuat Phat.

B2

Khoi dong 60 giay

Khi MC doc xong lenh bat dau, bam nut [Bat dau 60s] (nut mau xanh duong). Dong ho chay nguoc, nhac phat va cau hoi 1 hien len.

B3

Cham dung / sai

Neu thi sinh tra loi dung: Bam nut [DUNG (+10d)] (nut mau xanh la). He thong tu dong cong 10 diem va nhay sang cau tiep theo.
Neu thi sinh tra loi sai: Bam nut [SAI / BO QUA] (nut mau do). He thong bo qua khong cong diem va nhay sang cau tiep theo.

B4

Ket thuc luot thi

Dong ho ve 00s se tu dong bao het gio. Bam sang the thi sinh tiep theo de thuc hien luot thi moi.

4. VONG 2: RA KHOI (TANG TOC - THANG DIEM 40 - 30 - 20 - 10)

Gom 4 cau hoi kem clip/hinh anh. Ca 4 thi sinh cung suy nghi trong 30 giay. Thang diem ap dung:

Thu tu thoi gian nop bai dung Diem cong Nut bam tren Controller Quy tac ap dung
Dung nhanh nhat (Thu 1) +40 diem Nut [+40] hoac Nut Cham diem tu dong Thoi gian nop ngan nhat
Dung nhanh thu 2 +30 diem Nut [+30] hoac Nut Cham diem tu dong Nhanh thu nhi
Dung nhanh thu 3 +20 diem Nut [+20] hoac Nut Cham diem tu dong Nhanh thu ba
Dung nhanh thu 4 +10 diem Nut [+10] hoac Nut Cham diem tu dong Nhanh thu tu

Quy trinh 6 buoc dieu khien moi cau hoi Ra Khoi:

B1

Chon cau hoi

Bam chon nut [Cau 1] (hoac Cau 2, Cau 3, Cau 4) tren thanh chon cau hoi.

B2

Phat clip / Hien cau hoi

Bam nut [Phat clip / Hien cau hoi] de trinh chieu video hoac hinh anh len may chieu.

B3

Bat dau 30 giay dem nguoc

Bam nut [Bat dau 30s] (nut mau xanh dam). Dong ho dem nguoc va 4 thi sinh nhap dap an.

B4

Hien dap an thi sinh

Khi het gio, bam nut [Hien dap an thi sinh] de mo 4 khung dap an va thoi gian nop (vi du: 04.15s) len may chieu.

B5

Cham diem tu dong thong minh

Bam nut [Cham diem (40-30-20-10)]. Hop thoai xuat hien liet ke san 4 thi sinh theo thu tu nop nhanh nhat. Nguoi dieu khien chi can nhap so thi sinh tra loi dung (vi du: 1, 3) roi bam OK. He thong tu dong cong 40d cho nguoi nhanh nhat, 30d cho nguoi tiep theo.

B6

Hien bang diem

Bam nut [Diem TS] de may chieu hien thi bang tong diem sau cau hoi.

5. VONG 3: VUOT SONG (THANG DIEM 50 - 40 - 30 - 20 - 10)

Vong thi gom 4 cau hoi hang ngang, 1 cau hoi trung tam va 1 Tu khoa / Dap an vong thi.

Thoi diem tra loi Dap an vong thi Diem cong Nut thao tac tren ban dieu khien Dieu kien thoi diem
Thoi diem 1 +50 diem Nut [+50] hoac [Cham DA vong thi (50-10d)] Truoc hoac trong khi mo Hang ngang 1
Thoi diem 2 +40 diem Nut [+40] hoac [Cham DA vong thi (50-10d)] Sau Hang ngang 1 / Truoc Hang ngang 2
Thoi diem 3 +30 diem Nut [+30] hoac [Cham DA vong thi (50-10d)] Sau Hang ngang 2 / Truoc Hang ngang 3
Thoi diem 4 +20 diem Nut [+20] hoac [Cham DA vong thi (50-10d)] Sau Hang ngang 3 / Truoc Hang ngang 4
Thoi diem 5 +10 diem Nut [+10] hoac [Cham DA vong thi (50-10d)] Sau Hang ngang 4 / O trung tam
Dung cau hoi Hang ngang +10 diem Nut [+10] hoac [Cong 10d ca 4 TS] Cong cho thi sinh tra loi dung

Thao tac khi co thi sinh bam chuong tra loi Dap an vong thi:

  • Khi thi sinh bam chuong, chuong reo to va tai o cua thi sinh do xuat hien chu DO hien thi thoi gian bam chuong (vi du: TS2 bam chuong giay thu 15.20S).
  • Bam nut [Cham DA vong thi (50-10d)] tren thanh dieu khien:
    1. Nhap so thu tu thi sinh tra loi dung (1, 2, 3 hoac 4).
    2. Nhap moc thoi diem (1 la 50d, 2 la 40d, 3 la 30d, 4 la 20d, 5 la 10d).
    3. Bam OK -> He thong tu dong cong diem.
  • Ngoai ra co the bam truc tiep cac nut [+50], [+40], [+30], [+20], [+10] o ngay cot cua thi sinh do.
  • Bam nut [Mo tu khoa] de lat mo toan bo tu khoa va mo hinh anh trung tam.

6. VONG 4: VINH QUANG (VE DICH)

B1

Chon thi sinh va goi 3 cau hoi

Bam the thi sinh thi dau, sau do tich chon 3 cau hoi tu cac muc 10, 20 hoac 30 diem.

B2

Dat Ngoi sao hy vong

Neu thi sinh yeu cau dat ngoi sao truoc cau hoi, bam nut [Ngoi sao hy vong] (nut mau vang). Dung: gap doi diem; Sai: bi tru dung so diem cau hoi.

B3

Bat dau thoi gian va cham diem

Bam nut [Bat dau gio]. Dung: Bam [DUNG]; Sai: Bam [SAI]. Khi bam Sai, he thong tu dong mo chuong 5 giay cho 3 thi sinh con lai cuop quyen tra loi.

B4

Xu ly cuop diem

Thi sinh cuop diem bam truoc se duoc tra loi. Neu dung bam [DUNG CUOP DIEM] (nhan diem cau hoi); neu sai bam [SAI CUOP DIEM] (bi tru 50% diem).

7. XU LY SU CO KY THUAT VA THAO TAC KHAN CAP

  • Sua diem truc tiep: Nhap dup chuot (Double Click) vao o diem so cua thi sinh tren Controller, go so diem moi va nhan Enter de sua diem ngay lap tuc.
  • Mat mang may thi sinh: Bam nut [TS1], [TS2], [TS3] hoac [TS4] tren thanh Reload Role hoac cho thi sinh quet lai ma QR tren man hinh.
  • Tat am thanh khan cap: Bam nut [Tat am thanh] / [Stop Sound] o goc tren ben phai de ngat toan bo am thanh neu bi lap tieng.
  • Cau hoi phu: Chuyen sang tab [CAU HOI PHU] de bam chuong tranh ve di tiep khi co hai hay nhieu thi sinh bang diem.
